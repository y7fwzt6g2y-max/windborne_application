1. Semantle Strategy

A potential strategy for Semantle for someone who is unfilmilar with the game, at the most basic level, is to simply input ramdom words. After playing the game for a bit and understanding the similarity score that the game produces, a potential stratagy would be to start with very broad words, such as "thing" or "object." Once the similarity scores have been produced for these words, one should then proceed to list words that would be similar (or if you think of it as a hierarchy, under) those broader terms. Essentially, one is going from a coarse resolution to a fine resolution in order to see the "image" (word in this case) more clearly.
